About the Maker

Wood that tells a story.

I'm Adi — a craftsman with over a decade of experience in bespoke woodworking. Every piece I make begins with listening: to the client, to the grain of the wood, to the character of the space it will inhabit.

Working from my workshop in the UK, I combine traditional joinery techniques with considered modern design. No flat-pack shortcuts, no compromises on material quality.

— Adi Bespokemaker

What I craft for you

Breathing new life into antique and period pieces with sympathetic repairs and finishing.

How It Works

From first sketch to finished piece.

01

Consultation

We meet at your space. I listen, sketch, and measure. No jargon, no pressure — just an honest conversation about what you want.

01 / 08

02

Site Survey

Precise measurements of every wall, alcove, and awkward angle. Accuracy here means nothing needs adjusting at installation.

02 / 08

03

Design & Quote

Detailed drawings and a clear, fixed quote. You see exactly what you're getting before a single tool is lifted.

03 / 08

04

Material Selection

We choose the timber together — species, grain direction, finish. Every material decision is made with longevity and beauty in equal measure.

04 / 08

05

Workshop Build

Work begins in my workshop using quality UK-sourced timber. Traditional joinery techniques — no shortcuts, no flat-pack compromises.

05 / 08

06

Finishing

Hand-sanding, priming, painting or oiling — depending on the species and look you've chosen. The finish is what the eye sees first.

06 / 08

07

Installation

I fit everything myself. No subcontracting, no strangers in your home. The same hands that built it fit it.

07 / 08

08

Aftercare

A 5-year warranty on all work. If anything isn't right, I come back and fix it — no quibble, no charge. That's the promise.

08 / 08
